Nexus Commands

Explore the commands available with Nexus.

$help
Shows the Nexus help menu.
$ping
Check Nexus latency.
$botinfo
Shows information about Nexus.
$serverinfo
Shows information about the server.
$userinfo
Shows information about a user.
$avatar
Shows a user's avatar.
$clear
Deletes messages from a channel.
$kick
Kicks a member from the server.
$ban
Bans a member from the server.
$unban
Unbans a member.
$timeout
Times out a member.
$untimeout
Removes a member's timeout.
$warn
Warns a member.
$warnings
Shows a member's warnings.
$lock
Locks a channel.
$unlock
Unlocks a channel.
$slowmode
Changes channel slowmode.
$announce
Creates an announcement.
$poll
Creates a poll.
$embed
Creates an embed message.
$say
Makes Nexus send a message.
$afk
Set your AFK status.
$ticket
Opens the ticket system.
$close
Closes a ticket.
$add
Adds a member to a ticket.
$remove
Removes a member from a ticket.
$setup
Sets up Nexus features.
$config
Configure Nexus.
